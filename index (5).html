Common questions

Quick answers before you order.

Which phones work with the card?

Most modern iPhone and Android phones read NFC cards. Cards can also carry a QR code, so any phone camera can scan them.

Can I change the link later?

Yes. Message us and we will point your card to a new page. You do not need a new card.

Can I get my own design?

Yes. Your logo and colours go on the card. Choose Custom design in the form below.
